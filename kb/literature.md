## How to use

- Literature is evidence of level 1–2 at most (published, not AFS-validated for the customer's case).
  It may raise confidence (Q4), never decide the method on its own.
- Cite the DOI and the page. No quotes longer than one short sentence.
- Before a public release: only this list goes public, never the PDFs.
