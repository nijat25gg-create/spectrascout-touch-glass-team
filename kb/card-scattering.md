# Elastic light scattering, turbidity and reflection (UV-Vis-NIR)

## What it sees
Particles, droplets, bubbles, cells: turbidity, particle size trends, biomass, color and appearance of solids.

## Strengths
- Very robust and simple, silica fibers
- Good for biomass/cell density, crystallization onset, emulsions
- Fast

## Limits and typical exclusions
- Not chemically specific
- Depends on particle size, shape and refractive index
- Bubbles and fouling cause artefacts

## Probes and fibers
Backscatter and transmission turbidity probes, reflection probes.

## Typical in-line uses
- Biomass or optical density in fermentation
- Crystallization onset and nucleation
- Emulsion stability, color of solids

## How it maps to engine features (draft for Yarik)
analyte_type=particles/biomass → bonus; turbidity=high → this is the signal, not noise
